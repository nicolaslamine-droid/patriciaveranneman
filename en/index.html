04 — About

Patricia
Veranneman

Through painting and ceramics, Patricia Veranneman explores the traces of time, fragments of memory and the transformations of matter. Her work often starts from what already exists: an old photograph, a forgotten object, a found element, a form that carries a story within it. These fragments are not reproduced — they are displaced, transformed, recomposed, until they become something else.

Her paintings sometimes begin from old photographs: family pictures, anonymous scenes, memories of which only a trace remains. Moving from photograph to painting introduces a distance. Outlines fade, details disappear; what was a precise image becomes an impression, a presence, sometimes an absence.

Ceramics take up these same questions through material and volume. Old elements, found or carrying a story, are joined to the clay and set against a new form: neither kept intact nor quite erased. The pieces then hold several times at once, between relic and creation.
